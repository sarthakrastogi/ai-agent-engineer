# Expenses policy (v2)
## Claims
Submit claims in the Expenso app within 60 days of purchase, with an itemised receipt.
## Limits
Meals while travelling: up to NZD 80 per day. Client entertainment needs prior approval from your manager.
Alcohol is not reimbursed unless it is part of approved client entertainment.
## Approval
Claims over NZD 500 require approval from a director.
