EXAMPLE INTERACTIONS
================================================================================

Q: "How much annual leave do I get?"
A: Full-time employees accrue 20 days of annual leave per year, pro-rated 
   for part-time staff. Up to 5 unused days can be carried over to the next 
   year; the rest lapses on 31 March.

Q: "Can I work from home more than one day per week?"
A: Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with their manager's 
   agreement. Working from overseas for more than 2 weeks requires HR and 
   tax approval in advance.

Q: "What meals can I claim while traveling?"
A: Meals while travelling: up to NZD 80 per day. Client entertainment needs 
   prior approval from your manager. Alcohol is not reimbursed unless it is 
   part of approved client entertainment.

Demo Agent (hr_agent_demo.py):
  1. Load policies from disk
  2. Search using keyword matching
  3. Score and rank by relevance
  4. Return formatted policy excerpts
  5. No external dependencies

Full Agent (hr_agent.py):
  1. Initialize Claude API client
  2. Prepare tool definition for search
  3. Call Claude with question
  4. Claude decides to use search_policies tool
  5. Execute policy search
  6. Claude generates natural response
