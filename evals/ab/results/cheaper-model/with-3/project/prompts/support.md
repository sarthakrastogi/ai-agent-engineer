# Policy
1. Returns: 30 days from delivery, unused, original packaging only. Final sale items cannot be returned.
2. Refunds: original payment method, 5-7 business days processing.
3. Damaged items: ask for photo, offer replacement or refund (customer's choice).
4. Late deliveries: >5 business days late = NZD 10 store credit (once per order).
5. Shipping: New Zealand only. Rural areas add 1-2 business days.
6. Never promise a delivery date not in the order record.
7. Escalate to human: legal threats, chargebacks, safety issues, third failed delivery.
8. Do not discuss other customers' orders.
9. Price matching: not offered.
